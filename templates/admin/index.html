AI Virtual Clothing (cloth-v4)

Endpoint: /s2s/v2.0/task/cloth-v4

Model Photo Constraints

  • Posture: Standing or waist-up facing forward. Single subject.
  • Framing: Subject must occupy 75%–85%+ of the image area.
  • Lighting: Balanced daylight or studio fill. Avoid harsh side-shadows.
  • Arms: Keep arms slightly away from torso so garment boundary is clear.

Garment Image Constraints

  • Background: Clean solid white, neutral studio, or transparent PNG.
  • Display: Ghost mannequin or clean flat-lay without heavy folds.
  • Categories: dresses, upper_body, lower_body, outerwear, or auto.
💇‍♀️

Hairstyle Transfer & Color

Endpoints: /hair-transfer & /hair-color

Hairstyle Reference Standards

  • Angle: Direct frontal or gentle 3/4 portrait angle.
  • Contrast: Hair should contrast clearly against background for clean neural extraction.
  • Forehead & Hairline: Fully unobstructed, no hats, glasses, or headwear.

Color Pattern Support

  • Supports single-tone full color and dual-tone gradient Ombré blending.
  • Hex color codes with intensity and shine control (0–100).
👁️

Eye Color & Contact Lens VTO

Endpoint: /s2s/v2.0/task/eye-color-vto

Lens Asset Specification

  • Format: 32-bit Transparent PNG with alpha channel around iris.
  • Pattern: Centered circular iris pattern with clear pupil hole.
  • Dimensions: 200×200px to 600×600px square.
  • Effect parameters: Intensity (1–100), Enlargement (0–100), Skin smoothing strength.
✨

Skin & Tone Diagnostics

Endpoints: /skin-analysis & /skin-tone-analysis

Diagnostic Portrait Criteria

  • Facial Visibility: Full face uncovered (cheeks, forehead, chin).
  • Lighting: Diffused natural daylight. No tinted sunglasses or filters.
  • Expression: Neutral, eyes looking at camera.
  • 16 Metrics: Detects acne, firmness, radiance, tear troughs, dark circles, oiliness, and undertone.
